## 4. The problem

**Heading:** Compliance is a list of deadlines and documents. Most providers track it in spreadsheets.

- A reportable incident has a clock. For some incidents the NDIS Commission must be notified within 24 hours of awareness. For unauthorised restrictive practice, the report is due within 5 business days, or 24 hours if harm occurred.
- Worker screening checks expire. A lapsed check often shows up only when the roster is built.
- Policies go out of date when the Practice Standards or your services change.

**Line below:** Hauskeep puts each of these on one list, with a date and an owner.

## 5. How it works

**Heading:** Three steps. About an hour to set up.

1. **Tell us about your business.** Registration groups, services, states and team size. No participant details.
2. **Get your gap list.** Hauskeep maps your answers to the NDIS Practice Standards indicators and shows which evidence you hold, which you partly hold, and which are missing.
3. **Work the list.** Policies are drafted for you to edit and approve. Incidents start a deadline clock. Worker checks and training send reminders before they expire.

**Note under the steps:** Every AI-drafted policy is labelled "draft, not approved" until a named person approves it.

## 6. What Hauskeep does and does not do

| Hauskeep does | Hauskeep does not |

## 9. FAQ

**Is Hauskeep a certification or an audit?**
No. It is a readiness checklist. It shows gaps and deadlines against the NDIS Practice Standards. Certification and audit decisions are made by the NDIS Commission or its approved auditors.

**Who writes the policies?**
Hauskeep drafts them from templates and your business profile. A named person in your organisation reviews, edits and approves each one. The draft is labelled until it is approved.

**Does it lodge reports for me?**
No. It tells you when a report is due and holds the content you need. You lodge the report in the NDIS Commission portal yourself.

**Which portal?**
Incidents lodged from 12 October 2026 go through the NDIS Commission's new incident portal. Hauskeep shows which portal applies to each incident. It is not the NDIA's myplace provider portal, which handles claims and payments.

**Does it handle claims or invoices?**
Not in this version.

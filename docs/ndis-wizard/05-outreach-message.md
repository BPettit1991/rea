Status: Draft v0.1, not sent
Date: 2026-10-22
Sender placeholders: [Your name], [Organisation], [Product name]. Fill in after the brand decision (07-brand-options.md).

## 1. Rules before sending

1. This is research, not marketing. Do not pitch the product, price, or pilot in the first message. The interview script keeps the pitch for the end of the call.
2. Send only to registered NDIS providers, found through the public NDIS provider register or the provider's own published contact details. Do not buy or scrape contact lists.
3. Spam Act 2003: a research invitation is not a commercial message, but treat the rules as if it were. Identify yourself, give a real contact, and offer an opt-out in every email. Confirm this with counsel (06-legal-scoping-brief.md, section 3).
4. Do not mention the simulated interview data (the Linkhaus rehearsal file). Do not quote any interview to recruit.
5. Do not claim the product is compliant, certified, endorsed by the NDIS Commission, or affiliated with the NDIA.
6. Do not ask for participant names, NDIS numbers, or incident details in any message.
7. Log every send: date, provider, channel, response. Stop after one follow-up.

## 2. Screener (answer before booking)

| Question | Qualifies if |
|---|---|
| Are you a registered NDIS provider? | Yes |
| Roughly how many staff do you have? | 1 to 150 |
| What is your role? | Owner, director, coordinator, quality or compliance lead, or general manager |
| Which state or territory is your main operation in? | NSW or VIC for the first wave (scope D2) |
| Do you handle compliance work yourself or with a consultant? | Either. Record which |

Quota for the first 10 interviews: 5 small (1 to 30 staff) and 5 medium (31 to 150 staff). Balance states: at least 3 NSW and 3 VIC.

## 3. Email 1: invitation (send first)

**Subject:** A 45-minute research call on NDIS compliance work

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm [Your name] from [Organisation]. We are researching how small and medium NDIS providers handle policies, incident reporting, worker checks and pricing. I came across [Provider name] while looking at registered providers in [State].
>
> I'd like to learn how your team does this today. It's a 45-minute call, by phone or video, at a time that suits you. This is research only. I won't pitch you anything on the call, and you won't be asked to buy anything.
>
> We will record the call only if you agree, and we will delete the recording after transcription. We won't ask for participant names or any personal details about the people you support.
>
> Would a time in the next two weeks suit you? If not, just reply to say so and I won't follow up.
>
> Thanks,
> [Your name]
> [Role], [Organisation]
> [Phone] | [Email]
>
> You can reply "no thanks" at any time and I will not contact you again.

## 4. Short message (LinkedIn or phone script)

> Hi [Name], I'm researching how NDIS providers handle policies, incidents and worker checks. Could I book 45 minutes of your time? Research only, no sales pitch, and I'll delete any recording. Happy to send more detail first.

## 5. Follow-up (send once, 5 business days after email 1, only if no reply)

**Subject:** Re: A 45-minute research call on NDIS compliance work

> Hi [Name], following up on my note last week. If now isn't a good time, no problem. Reply "no thanks" and I'll close the loop. Otherwise I'm happy to work around your roster.
>
> [Your name]

Do not send a third message.

## 6. Confirmation email (after a time is booked)

**Subject:** Confirming our call on [date and time]

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for making time. Our call is booked for [date, time, time zone], by [phone or video link].
>
> Before we talk, here is what to expect:
>
> - This is research. We will ask about how your team manages policies, incidents, worker checks and pricing, and about the time and cost of that work.
> - We will ask whether you agree to a recording before we start. If you say no, we take notes by hand.
> - We will not ask for participant names or identifying details. If you mention one, we will not write it down.
> - You can skip any question or stop at any time.
> - After the call we will send you a short written summary. It will not name you or your organisation outside our team.
>
> If anything changes, reply to this email.
>
> [Your name]

## 7. Thank-you email (after the call, within 2 business days)

**Subject:** Thank you for your time

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thank you for talking with me today. Your examples were useful, and I've noted what you said about [one specific point from the call, in their words, with no identifying detail].
>
> We will send a short summary once we have spoken with the other providers. It will not name you or your organisation.
>
> Would you be open to a short follow-up in 4 to 6 weeks? Separately, we are looking for a small number of providers to try an early version. If you would like to know more, I can send details. Saying no now has no effect on our research.
>
> [Your name]

Send the pilot question as a separate message, and only if the interviewee scored the concept 6 or above (script question 21). Do not attach a price or a contract on the first send.
